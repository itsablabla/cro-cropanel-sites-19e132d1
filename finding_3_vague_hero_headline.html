⚠ Vague headlineThe headline is emotionally vague and fails to communicate the value proposition or address the visitor's intent to buy affordable glasses.
ProblemThe hero headline 'A fresh dose of nostalgia' is emotionally vague and fails to communicate the core value proposition or address the visitor's intent to buy affordable glasses. Visitors arrive with a specific need (affordable eyewear) and are met with a poetic but unclear message, causing confusion and potential bounce. The CTA 'Shop Putney' is also ambiguous, not clearly indicating what the visitor will find. This lack of clarity reduces the likelihood of engagement and conversion.
RECOMMENDED — Value-prop headline
Homepage Hero

Stylish glasses, starting at $95

Discover affordable, stylish eyewear that fits your budget and your look.

Shop eyeglassesShop all '90s styles
✓ Value-prop headlineLeads with the value proposition (affordable, stylish eyewear) and a clear CTA that matches the visitor's intent to browse or buy.
FixReplace the vague headline with a value-prop headline that clearly states the benefit: 'Stylish glasses, starting at $95'. This immediately communicates affordability and style, addressing the visitor's intent. Change the primary CTA to 'Shop eyeglasses' to match the browsing intent and reduce ambiguity. Keep the secondary CTA for those interested in specific styles, but the primary message now leads with the value proposition.
