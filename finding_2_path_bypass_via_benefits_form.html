⚠ Benefits form bypassThe prominent benefits form sends users to insurance verification instead of product discovery.
ProblemThe homepage prominently features a 'Check my benefits' form that collects personal information (name, DOB, zip) and submits to insurance verification. This creates a parallel path that diverts users away from the primary quiz-based product discovery flow. As a result, users who intend to shop may be funneled into a non-purchase activity, increasing friction and reducing conversion. The form's early placement and visual weight compete with the quiz CTA, confusing the primary call-to-action.
RECOMMENDED — Quiz-first CTA
Homepage

Need to check benefits? Check my benefits

✓ Quiz-first pathPrimary CTA now leads to product discovery, reducing friction.
FixDe-emphasize the benefits checker by removing it from the primary homepage area and relocating it to a secondary location, such as the footer or a dedicated page. Replace the prominent form with a clear, single primary CTA: 'Start with a quiz'. This simplifies the user journey, reduces cognitive load, and aligns with the main conversion goal of product discovery. The benefits checker remains accessible for users who need it, but no longer competes for attention.
